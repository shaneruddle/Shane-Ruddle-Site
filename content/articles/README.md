# Articles

Each `<slug>.md` file here is published at `https://shaneruddle.com/articles/<slug>` on the next deploy
(any push to `main`). Use lowercase words separated by hyphens for the filename.

```
---
title: Article title
description: One or two sentences for Google and social previews (max ~155 characters).
category: Car Rental   # My Advice | Property | Car Rental | Pattaya News
date: 2026-10-19
image: /images/articles/example.webp   # optional
draft: true                            # optional; hides the article
---

Article body in Markdown.
```
