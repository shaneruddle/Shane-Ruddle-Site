Retiring to Pattaya can be fantastic, but it's easy to get stale. My advice is simple: have two or three hobbies, and make sure they cover three things. Something active, something social and something relaxing.

## Why people end up in the bars

A lot of people who drift into the bar scene never really had hobbies. Their life was their work or their family. When they come here, the work has gone and the family is back home, or the kids have already flown the nest. So they can feel a bit lonely.

Maybe they tried golf and found it too hard, or just didn't enjoy it. At a certain age, football or running might not be realistic. If you're not very fit or not the sporty type, you might think, "Well, what's left for me?"

A lot of what's left involves sitting around and talking, and the bar scene is the easiest version of that. You set your own schedule, you can move between bars, you can talk to lots of different people, and it's relatively cheap here. Nobody cuts you off either. You could do it from the moment you wake up until you go to sleep, if that was your thing. That's why it's attractive, and that's exactly why it's a trap.

## 1. Something active

One hobby needs to be physical, depending on your ability. Golf, padel, running or walking all count. The point is to keep moving regularly. More in [staying fit in Pattaya](/articles/staying-fit-in-pattaya).

## 2. Something social

You definitely need something where you meet people, whether they're new faces or people you already know. Golf and padel cover active and social in one go. If your active hobby is something like walking, which isn't very sociable, you'll need to add something.

If you're not sporty, there's still plenty to do. There are clubs, like chess clubs, and meetups all over the city. There are events too, from wine tasting to salsa dancing. Have a look through Facebook events or Google, and you'll find lots of chances to meet people.

## 3. Something relaxing

The third should be a way to wind down, and you should do it regularly. That might mean going to the beach to read a book, or getting a massage. Whatever works for you.

## Then double down

If you can combine some of those, great. Once you've got one of each, double up: two active pursuits, two social ones and two ways to relax. When you've ticked those off, go again and make it three of each.

## My take

Pattaya has everything you need for a full, healthy retirement. You just have to choose it, rather than letting the easy option choose you.
