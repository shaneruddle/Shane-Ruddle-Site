Bang Saen is only an hour from Pattaya, so you could do it as a day trip. But whenever I go, I stay one night. There's enough to keep you busy for a couple of days, and doing it all in one day means leaving early and getting back late.

## A very Thai beach town

Almost everyone in Bang Saen is Thai. It's very much a Thai destination. The beach is nice and clean, and it feels like going back in time a little.

## Where to stay

I always stay at the Amari Bangsaen, right on the beachfront. I think it's the best hotel in Bang Saen. It's reasonably priced, the breakfast is good and it's great for kids. There are plenty of other hotels, and they're inexpensive.

## What to do

- **The aquarium.** It's run by Burapha University's Institute of Marine Science. Entry is around ฿200 per person, and it takes an hour and a half or so to get round. Definitely worth a visit.
- **The monkeys.** At Khao Sam Muk there's an area with just under a hundred wild monkeys. You buy fruit and corn from the sellers for ฿10 or ฿20, put it on your car, and the monkeys jump on and eat it, a bit like a safari park back home. The kids love it.
- **The mangrove walk.** A short drive out of town. You walk along a wooden boardwalk right at the water's edge. Worth the trip.
- **Cycling.** Rent a bicycle and ride along the beach. It's flat, so it's easy going.

## The seafood

Seafood is a must. There are plenty of really good seafood restaurants, some of them modern, and it's good quality and good value. If you don't eat seafood, there are plenty of other options.
