Running is booming in Thailand. I was walking through Chatuchak Park in Bangkok recently and there were thousands of people running, jogging and doing aerobics. If you want to join in, Pattaya has some great places to start.

## Chatuchak Park

I'd seen the park on the map on my way to the weekend market, but had never been in. It had a really nice atmosphere. Almost everyone had the proper gear: running shoes, singlets, smart watches and music. It's clearly become a big trend.

## Pattaya's version

We have something similar in Pattaya at Mabprachan Lake. Go any day of the week and there are hundreds of people running around it. It's not quite Chatuchak, but it has the same feel.

## My running

I still run, though not as much as I used to. I've done a lot of half marathons around Thailand, including Pattaya, [Bang Saen](/articles/bang-saen-from-pattaya), Chiang Mai and Bangkok. At the moment I'm playing padel every day, and I find it hard to run and play padel on the same day, so the running has taken a back seat. (More on [how I stay fit in Pattaya](/articles/staying-fit-in-pattaya).)

## Where to run in Pattaya

- **Pratumnak Hill.** Around Buddha Hill, with views over the bay.
- **Mabprachan Lake.** Flat, popular, and you won't be running alone.

## When to run

You have to beat the heat. Either go early, around 6:30 to 7am, or in the evening, around 5:30 to 6pm. I prefer the evening. The atmosphere is much better.

If you've been thinking about starting, now's a good time. You'll find plenty of company. And if team sport is more your thing, here's [playing football in Pattaya](/articles/playing-football-in-pattaya).
