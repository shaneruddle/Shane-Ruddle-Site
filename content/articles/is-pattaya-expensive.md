People ask me all the time whether Pattaya is expensive. My honest answer is no. It's more expensive than a lot of Thailand, but compared with almost anywhere else in the world that's worth living in, it isn't expensive at all.

## How much does a single person need per month in Pattaya?

Take a single man living in a one-bed condo. Here's roughly what it looks like:

- **Rent:** around ฿18,000 a month for a one-bed condo.
- **Food:** a meal costs a couple of hundred baht, or about ฿300 with a drink. Eat out every meal and that's around ฿1,000 a day, so about ฿30,000 a month. Cook some of your own meals and it drops a lot.
- **Getting around:** a few thousand baht a month to rent a motorbike. Buy one and getting around becomes very cheap.

That comes to roughly ฿50,000 to ฿55,000 a month, eating out every day.

## How much does it cost for a family?

For families, the big cost is schooling. Thai schools are relatively cheap, but most foreign families send their children to an international school, and that pushes monthly costs up a lot. Budget around ฿30,000 a month per child, and that's at the lower end. I've written more about schools and family life in [Is Pattaya Good for Families?](/articles/is-pattaya-good-for-families)

## What's more expensive than people expect?

**Going out.** Entertainment and drinking aren't as cheap as people think. You can do it cheaply, but depending on your appetite, it can get expensive.

**Visas and admin.** Visas, paperwork and the other admin you have to do here can add up.

**Health insurance.** I always recommend getting it, and it can be expensive. Here's more on [healthcare and insurance in Pattaya](/articles/healthcare-in-pattaya).

## What's cheap in Pattaya?

Getting around is cheap, and eating out is cheap ([here's where I eat](/articles/where-a-restaurant-owner-eats-in-pattaya)). And if you enjoy simple things like going to the beach or walking, it costs next to nothing. That's true anywhere, but here it's more enjoyable.

## How do you live well in Pattaya without overspending?

Manage your finances and keep an eye on your expenses. And take care in relationships: choosing the wrong partner can end up costing you a lot of money.
