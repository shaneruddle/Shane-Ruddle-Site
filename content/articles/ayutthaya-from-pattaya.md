I've been to Ayutthaya twice, both times as a stopover on a road trip north to Chiang Mai. That's how I'd recommend doing it. From Pattaya it isn't worth driving up, staying one night and coming straight back. Make it the first stop on a bigger trip.

## Getting there

It's about three hours' drive from Pattaya. If that's too long in one go, Bangkok is the obvious place to break the journey. From Bangkok itself, it's only an hour or so north. If you need a car, [Pattaya Rent a Car](https://pattayarentacar.com) can help, and before you go, read [driving in Pattaya](/articles/driving-in-pattaya).

## The historical park

Ayutthaya was Thailand's old capital, and the historical park, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is the main reason to go. I walked and cycled around it, which is the best way to see it. You'll see the ruins of the old city, destroyed when the Burmese army sacked it in 1767, and the moats around the temples that were meant to protect the people living there.

If you're a history buff, or a couple looking for something different, it's a good trip. One night is enough.

## Carry on to Sukhothai

Further north, [Sukhothai](/articles/sukhothai-from-pattaya) is a great next stop. It's also very historic, with its own historical parks and water around the temples, and it's very clean with a really nice, chilled-out vibe.

From there, you can carry on to [Chiang Mai](/articles/chiang-mai-from-pattaya). Doing it as one road trip, with a night or two at each stop, is far better than trying to do any of them on their own from Pattaya.
