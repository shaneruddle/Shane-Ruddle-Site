When I first arrived in Pattaya more than 20 years ago, you hardly saw Thais from Bangkok here. I think the turning point was the 2011 floods. Bangkok people came down to escape them, discovered a Pattaya they didn't expect, and they've been coming ever since. In my view, they've helped make Pattaya the city it is today.

## Bangkok Thais never used to come

Back then, Pattaya was relatively underdeveloped. I'm not sure exactly why Bangkok Thais didn't come. Maybe they went elsewhere, like Khao Yai or Hua Hin. But generally speaking, you didn't see them here.

## The floods

Then came the 2011 floods. Bangkok was in chaos and people couldn't get around. Many Bangkok Thais, especially the wealthier ones, left the city for a month or so until things got back to normal.

One of the places they went was Pattaya, because we're by the sea and we don't get floods like that. Many had either never been here or hadn't been for a very long time, and I think a lot of them were surprised by how much it had developed.

## Condos on a credit card

I wasn't in real estate at the time, but friends told me Bangkok Thais were coming down and buying condos over a weekend, simply putting them on their card. Back then you could pick up a condo for something like a million baht.

It really transformed the property market. And it didn't stop when the floods ended. Over the years, they've kept coming, more and more often.

## Raising the standard

The reason I keep saying Bangkok Thais specifically is that many of them have money. As they started coming regularly, they raised the standard for a lot of restaurants here, and prices went up with it.

Over the last ten years or so, I've also noticed many of them opening businesses here. Restaurants that started in Bangkok have opened branches in Pattaya. First they bought property, and the natural next step was business.

## What it looks like now

My own businesses don't serve that many Thai customers, so it doesn't affect us directly. But you only have to go out on a holiday weekend to see it.

I was leaving Terminal 21 recently, and it was absolutely packed. The queue at Starbucks was about ten deep, and every restaurant had people in it. Part of that was the start of high season, part was the fireworks festival bringing people in from neighbouring provinces, and part was Bangkok Thais down for their usual weekend break.

## My take

Foreign visitors built Pattaya's reputation, but Bangkok Thais have done a lot to shape the modern city: the property market, the restaurants, the malls and the busy weekends. The 2011 floods were a disaster for Bangkok, but they opened a lot of eyes to Pattaya.
