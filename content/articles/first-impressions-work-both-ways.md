When you walk into a business, you're judging it: whether you trust it, whether you'll spend your money there. But in car rental and property especially, the business is judging you too. If we feel you're going to be more trouble than you're worth, we reserve the right not to serve you, and we use it.

## The lads who didn't get their bikes

At Pattaya Rent a Car, we turn customers away regularly, based purely on how they behave when they walk in.

One group sticks in my mind. Six to eight young lads bowled into our office, which isn't big. They were loud, boisterous and messing about. My staff were dealing with them, and I was in another part of the office watching.

Nothing about how they acted told me they'd treat our bikes with respect. The risk was far too high. So I came over and told them straight: we're not going to rent to you today.

I also gave them some advice. When you go to the next rental place, take it a bit more seriously and act a bit more respectfully. Otherwise, you'll get the same answer there.

## Why it matters

When we rent you a car or a motorbike, we're handing over something worth a lot of money and trusting you to bring it back in one piece. When we take on a property client, we're committing time and our reputation. How you behave in the first five minutes tells us a lot about how the next few weeks will go.

We work hard to make a good first impression on you. It's only fair that it works the other way too.

## What makes a good impression

- **Be polite and patient**, even if there's a queue or a delay.
- **Take the process seriously.** Read what you're signing, have your documents ready, and ask sensible questions.
- **Respect the staff and the premises.** It's a workplace, not a bar.
- **Be honest** about your experience, your plans and your budget.

## My take

Most customers are great, and we're happy to help them. But turning away the occasional customer who looks like trouble protects our staff, our vehicles and every other customer. If you want the best service in Pattaya, the easiest way to get it is to make a good first impression yourself.
