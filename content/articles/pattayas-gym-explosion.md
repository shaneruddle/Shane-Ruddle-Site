If you've been to Pattaya recently, you'll have noticed the gyms. They're everywhere, and Pratumnak in particular has more than you'd ever expect for an area its size. Over the last ten years or so, there's been a real explosion of them.

## How many are there?

Just in Pratumnak, there are at least half a dozen gyms. When I used to train, I went to Muscle Factory, one of the biggest and best in the area. I chose it because it was clean, well kept, and within walking distance of where I work. But there are at least five others close by.

And it's not just gyms. There's been a similar boom in saunas and steam rooms, with at least four in Pratumnak alone.

## Why the boom?

It isn't fitness tourism. I think it's down to the digital nomads and the younger crowd Pattaya now attracts.

Younger people generally look after themselves more than previous generations did. Fewer of them are drinking and smoking, and more are training and eating well. That's a worldwide trend, and Pattaya has followed it. I've seen the same thing with [the running boom in Thailand](/articles/running-is-booming-in-thailand).

## What it costs

Rough prices in Pratumnak. These vary from place to place, so check with the gym first:

- **Gym day pass:** about 150 baht
- **Gym monthly membership:** about 1,500 baht for a good mid-range gym
- **Sauna and steam, per day:** about 250 baht

## My take

The old image of Pattaya was beer bars and late nights. That's still here, but there's a whole other Pattaya now, one that starts the day in the gym and finishes it in the sauna. And it's a lot healthier.
