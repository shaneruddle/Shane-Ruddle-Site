Saunas aren't really my thing, so when my partner wanted to go to one recently, she went on her own.

Before she left, I warned her. In Pattaya especially, I told her, people would want to chat to her, and at the very least they'd stare, and not subtly.

That's exactly what happened. In the end, she left early because she felt a bit unsafe.

## It shouldn't be like that

She shouldn't have needed a warning, and she shouldn't have had to leave. Unfortunately, because of the image Pattaya has and the scene here, some men don't behave the way they should.

## Read the situation

Yes, Pattaya has its nightlife, and some women here are looking for male company. But that doesn't mean every Thai woman is. Most aren't. Someone at a sauna, a gym, a café or the beach is just getting on with her day.

So be smart about it. Read the situation, and treat people with a bit more care and respect.

## Know the law

I was talking to a tourist recently who had no idea about this, so it's worth spelling out. Since 30 December 2025, sexual harassment has been a specific criminal offence in Thailand. It doesn't need physical contact. The law covers five types of behaviour:

1. **Verbal:** sexual remarks or comments about someone's body.
2. **Gestures:** leering, whistling or making kissing noises.
3. **Online:** obscene messages, images or harassing comments.
4. **Physical:** touching or hugging without consent.
5. **Abuse of power:** for example, a boss pressuring an employee.

Penalties start at up to a year in prison, a 20,000 baht fine, or both, and rise for repeat offences, public or online shaming, people in positions of authority and other serious cases.

Over the years, there have been stories of tourists who misread a situation, touched a woman who wasn't interested, and ended up arrested. As they should be.

*I'm not a lawyer. This is a summary, not legal advice.*

## Just be respectful

My message is simple. Be respectful. It doesn't matter whether someone shows any interest in you or not. Respect the culture, respect people whatever their nationality, and respect women. Just be a decent guy.

Most people here are. It's the few who aren't who make others feel uncomfortable, and who give the rest of us a bad name.

## My take

A bit of gentlemanly conduct costs nothing. Everyone should be able to relax somewhere without feeling watched or unsafe.
