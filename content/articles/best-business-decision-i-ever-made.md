The best business decision I ever made came straight after COVID. I saw a wave of demand coming before it reached Thailand, and we doubled the Pattaya Rent a Car fleet from about 50 cars to 100 in a year. More in [16 years of Pattaya Rent a Car](/articles/16-years-of-pattaya-rent-a-car) and [how my businesses got through COVID](/articles/how-my-businesses-got-through-covid).

## Spotting it abroad first

As the world came out of COVID, I kept reading reports from America and Europe. Car rental companies couldn't keep up with demand. They were investing heavily in bigger fleets and still falling short, and they could raise prices a lot. People had extra money, partly from government support, and everyone wanted to get out and travel.

The West was opening up a few months ahead of Asia, so I could see what was coming. I told my business partner at Pattaya Rent a Car we probably had three or four months before the same thing hit us.

## A perfect storm

Demand was about to go through the roof, but supply in Pattaya and Jomtien would be tight, because many competitors had shrunk their fleets during COVID. We'd kept most of ours. That gave us the chance to grow.

## Going all in

We reinvested everything we earned into more cars and went from around 50 to 100 in about 12 months.

The spike came as I'd predicted, and if anything it was stronger. What I didn't predict was how long it would last. I thought it might be one high season. It lasted three or four years and is only now levelling off to pre-COVID levels. More in [Pattaya's high season is getting shorter](/articles/pattayas-high-season-is-getting-shorter).

## The lesson

- **Try to look ahead.** You can't predict the future exactly, but some things are fairly obvious if you pay attention. Plan years out rather than just reacting.
- **When you spot something, back it with conviction.** Don't dip a toe in. Double down.
- **Every crisis brings an opportunity.** Look for it.
