In July 2026 I drove down to Koh Tao with my girlfriend. It's a long way from Pattaya, but it was worth it: sharks from our balcony, a once-in-a-lifetime dive for her, and a sea turtle for me on the very last day. Give it at least three or four nights.

## Getting there: break up the drive

We drove from Pattaya and stopped overnight in Hua Hin. I'd recommend breaking up the drive, because it's a long one, well over 500 km to the ferry at Chumphon. If you need a car, [Pattaya Rent a Car](https://pattayarentacar.com) can help, and before you go, read [driving in Pattaya](/articles/driving-in-pattaya).

In hindsight, Hua Hin is a bit too close to Pattaya to be the ideal halfway point. Prachuap Khiri Khan is closer to halfway. But there's a lot more to do in Hua Hin, so it depends what you want from the stop. More in [Pattaya vs Hua Hin](/articles/pattaya-vs-hua-hin).

## The ferry from Chumphon

We caught the ferry from Chumphon, and I was surprised how good it was. It's a fast passenger-only boat, no cars or bikes, and the crossing took about an hour and a half. It was smooth, with no real motion sickness.

We paid an extra ฿200 each for the VIP area, which is air-conditioned with comfortable seats. I'd recommend it, and we did the same on the way back.

## Sharks from the balcony

Our hotel was right on the shore. We opened the curtains and could see the whole bay, and we actually saw small sharks, about three feet long, swimming below our balcony. They were most likely blacktip reef sharks, which are common around Koh Tao and harmless to people.

## Her dive: a whale shark

The reason we went was so my girlfriend could go scuba diving. On one dive, her group of about a dozen people saw a whale shark. It was her first time diving that deep, so she assumed that was just what you see on a dive.

When they surfaced, everyone was high-fiving. Some of them had done over 200 dives and never seen one. Only once she was back on the boat did she realise how lucky she'd been.

## My days: a motorbike and coffee shops

Diving isn't really my thing. While she was out on dive trips, I rented a motorbike, explored the island, sat in coffee shops and did a bit of work. They were really nice, relaxing days. When she came back in the afternoons, we went snorkelling in the bay together, and explored some of the beaches, which were lovely.

## The turtle on the last day

We'd been hoping to see a turtle all week. On our last day, we'd just finished snorkelling and were walking back to our room when a couple still in the water shouted up that they were following a turtle in the bay.

We ran back down, put our masks back on and dived in, and there it was, a big sea turtle. For me, that was the highlight of the trip. For my girlfriend, after a whale shark, it was a bit of an anticlimax.

## The vibe

The crowd is very different from Pattaya. There are lots of backpackers and young Europeans, and a really nice, relaxed vibe.

## What I didn't like

The roads. Everyone seemed to be in a rush, speeding around on loud motorbikes. On the beaches you get the peace and quiet you'd expect on a tropical island, but away from them it wasn't peaceful at all. More in [scooter or car in Pattaya?](/articles/scooter-or-car-in-pattaya)

## How long to stay

Getting there takes some effort, with the drive and the ferry, so it's not a place for one or two nights. I'd stay at least three or four.
