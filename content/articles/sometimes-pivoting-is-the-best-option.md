Not every business works out the way you planned. Sometimes the answer isn't to push harder or to close the doors, but to change direction. I've pivoted several businesses over the years, and I'm thinking about doing it again now.

## Fairways: from golf balls to food, drink and a golf series

The biggest pivot I can remember was at Fairways Golf Range. The core business at the start was selling trays of golf balls. We soon realised that on its own, it wasn't enough.

So we moved into food and drink. Then we added a golf society and the Pattaya Amateur Golf Series. In each case we moved away from our original offering. I've written more about [how a monthly golf day built my business network](/articles/how-a-monthly-golf-day-built-my-business-network).

## London Pie and the cloud kitchen

Another example was our pie manufacturing company, London Pie. It wasn't doing well, so I turned the premises into a cloud kitchen.

That didn't work either. So I pivoted again and moved the cloud kitchen into central Pattaya, into The Golf Club, another business I had at the time. In the end I was able to get out of it by selling it. Not every pivot is a success. The full story is in [my worst business ever: the cloud kitchen](/articles/my-worst-business-ever-the-cloud-kitchen).

## How do you know when to pivot?

For me, it comes down to two things.

- **Is the concept improving or declining?** If the core business isn't going well, be honest about the direction it's heading.
- **Are bigger market forces working against you?** Sometimes it isn't your business that's the problem. It's the whole category.

If the core business isn't going well and I can't see a future for the concept, that's when I'd pivot.

## What I'm thinking about now

That's exactly the question I'm asking at Hemingways Lakeside. I'm starting to feel that the pub concept is in general decline, and I'm not sure it will get any easier over the next ten years.

It may be time to pivot towards something more niche, or towards something like wellness, which is trending at the moment. More on Lakeside in [my business failures](/articles/my-business-failures).
