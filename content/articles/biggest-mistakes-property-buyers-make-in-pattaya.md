After years running two property agencies, I'd say the biggest mistake buyers make isn't the property they choose. It's who they choose to buy it through. More in [buying a condo in Pattaya](/articles/buying-a-condo-in-pattaya).

## Buying through the wrong person

Plenty of people buy through a friend's girlfriend, someone they met in a bar, or someone they found on Facebook. There are lots of polished social media profiles posting property, and you have no idea whether that person has been in the business five minutes or five years.

Here's what goes wrong:

- **No office, no process.** When a tenant wants their deposit back and there's a dispute, there's nowhere for both sides to sit down and sort it out. Freelancers often meet clients in coffee shops. We've had to step in and clear up other people's mess as a favour to owners we know.
- **The Land Office.** Transfers can be complicated, and without experience they get messy very quickly.
- **Money into a personal account.** This is the big one. A freelancer often has no company and no company bank account. Sending your money to someone's personal account is extremely risky. It can also cause problems with the paperwork: to own a condo freehold as a foreigner, the money normally has to come in from abroad in your name, with the bank issuing a foreign exchange transaction (FET) certificate showing it's for the purchase.

## Do you need a lawyer?

You don't legally need a lawyer to buy property in Thailand. If you use a reputable agency, it will do the due diligence for you, including checking the building's foreign and Thai quota. Some buyers still like an independent lawyer for peace of mind, and that's their call.

## Lowballing and waiting for the market to turn

The second mistake I see all the time is putting in a low offer, having it turned down, and assuming nobody else wants the place. Buyers wait for the price to drop, then it sells to someone else.

We've had angry customers over the years who missed out on their dream property. They didn't realise it was being marketed by plenty of other agencies, not just ours. More in [selling a condo in Pattaya](/articles/selling-a-condo-in-pattaya).

## How to spot a reputable agency

- **Years in business.** A track record matters.
- **A real office.** Somewhere you can walk in and sit down.
- **Social proof.** Check their reviews, their website and their social media, the same way you'd check any company.
