I've written about difficult customers and [unfair one-star reviews](/articles/how-i-handle-an-unfair-one-star-review). But when I think about the customers I most enjoy serving, something jumps out. Across car rental, my restaurants and property, they're almost always business owners themselves. Maybe it's a coincidence, but I don't think so.

## Paul

One who always comes to mind is Paul, a Pattaya Rent a Car customer for many years. He was always polite, never asked for a discount and never expected us to bend over backwards. He was just a genuinely nice, easygoing customer.

Paul runs his own business. I think that's exactly why he sees things from our side, not just the customer's.

## What a great customer looks like

The best customers are polite and respectful to the staff, and they trust us. They don't quiz us on every insurance detail or what happens if this or that goes wrong. They've done their research, chosen to trust us, and know we'll do the right thing if anything happens.

They turn up, pay, take the car and bring it back, and everything is easy. That's how we like to do things too, so it's a mirror of our own approach. More in [renting a car in Pattaya](/articles/renting-a-car-in-pattaya) and [16 years of Pattaya Rent a Car](/articles/16-years-of-pattaya-rent-a-car).

Some become friends. Paul would always take time for a chat that had nothing to do with the rental: are you busy, how's the family. Just nice people.

## Why we don't give them discounts

We don't look after these customers with loyalty discounts. The only thing we do is make sure there's a vehicle ready whenever they need one.

Customers like Paul, or another regular, Steve, aren't price-conscious. Offer them 10% off and they wouldn't care. Being business owners themselves, what they actually want is to see us do well, stay in business and make money.

What they want is to be treated with respect, and to have their own friendliness matched. More in [customer service and online reviews](/articles/customer-service-and-online-reviews).

## It's the same in every business

Now I think about it, it isn't just Pattaya Rent a Car. The customers we most like serving in the restaurants, and the clients we most enjoy working with in property, so often turn out to run businesses of their own.

My guess is that they know what it takes. They know what it's like to deal with the public, to pay staff and to keep the lights on. So they're patient, they're fair, and they want you to succeed. If you run a business, it's worth remembering that the next time you're on the other side of the counter. More in [how I run seven businesses](/articles/how-i-run-seven-businesses).
