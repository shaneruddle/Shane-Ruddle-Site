I've run property agencies in Pattaya for years, and I've lived here for more than 20. Yet I've always rented my own home. People are often surprised by that, so here's why.

## Following the business

For a long time I was on my own, and I always liked to live close to my businesses. I still do. I think I got that from my granddad, who liked to live near his businesses, especially once he had more than one. Ideally, you want all your businesses in the same area and your home close to the main one.

As I bought and sold businesses and moved them around as opportunities came up, I wanted the flexibility to move with them. Renting gave me that.

## Life changes too

Then my circumstances changed. I went from being single to having a family, first one child and then two. Each time, renting meant I could end a contract and move somewhere more suitable, without being tied to a property.

## Easy to buy, hard to sell

There's another reason, and it comes from what I see every day in property. Buying a property in Thailand is very easy, maybe almost too easy. Selling one is difficult, and maybe a little too difficult. I've always been aware of that.

## Money working harder elsewhere

There was also the money. Say I had 10 million baht. Parking it in a property to save a few percent a year in rent never sat well with me, when I could put the same money into a business and perhaps make 20%.

## Would I buy now?

Now I'm more settled. Not in Thailand, because I've been settled here a very long time, but in my life. I can't see us having more children, and I'm not really looking for more businesses.

So yes, I'd definitely consider buying now. When the money isn't needed for the next business, buying a home starts to make a lot more sense.

*This is my own experience, not financial advice.*

## My take

Renting isn't dead money if it buys you flexibility while your life and your business are changing. Once things settle, that's the time to think about buying.
