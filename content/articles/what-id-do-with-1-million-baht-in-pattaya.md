If you'd just moved to Pattaya with ฿1 million and wanted to stay long term, I wouldn't buy property and I probably wouldn't start a business. I'd invest it in myself.

## Why not property or a business?

฿1 million isn't a lot of money these days. It isn't enough to buy property and make anything from it. You might be able to start a small service business with it, but that's high risk. More in [setting up your own business in Pattaya](/articles/setting-up-a-business-in-pattaya).

## Invest in yourself

I'd spend it on skills:

- **Fluent Thai.** It would help you land a job and help your future here. More in [learning Thai](/articles/learning-thai).
- **AI.** Subscribe to ChatGPT or Claude, whichever you prefer, and learn to use it properly. Here's [how I use AI in my businesses](/articles/how-i-use-ai-in-my-businesses).
- **Online marketing and business.**

Most of that learning is free or cheap nowadays, though you can pay for courses if you want to. That's my preference and it won't be everyone's, but if your goal is to stay in Pattaya long term and ฿1 million is all you have, that's what I'd do.

## It buys you time

The rest of the money buys you time to learn. To live here comfortably you need about ฿50,000 to ฿60,000 a month, with rent at ฿15,000 to ฿18,000 and the rest on eating out and day-to-day costs. So ฿1 million lasts around a year and a half with no other income. More in [is Pattaya expensive?](/articles/is-pattaya-expensive)

## Never spend it on nights out

The surest way to burn through it is entertainment: going out with friends and drinking. Even a cheap night out here costs a couple of thousand baht. If you get carried away, like I used to, you can easily spend ฿10,000 in a night. I haven't had a drink in over 10 years.

## If you insist on a business

If it's not a small service business you can start for under ฿1 million, I'd team up with someone else and take a smaller share of a much bigger pie. Say someone wants to open a restaurant that needs ฿10 million. You could buy 10%, and if the restaurant does well, you might hope for ฿15,000 to ฿20,000 a month. That would be a very good return, so go in knowing it could be less, or nothing at all. Before you do it, read [business partners in Pattaya](/articles/business-partners-in-pattaya).
