My real friends don't ask me for a discount, and I don't ask them for one. That's the simple rule, and it's why name-dropping me at the counter doesn't get anyone a cheaper price.

## "I know Shane"

It happens mostly at Pattaya Rent a Car. You can't really walk into Hemingways and haggle over your drink, but a car rental feels like somewhere you can ask for a deal.

Someone might have heard my name through a friend, or been told "if you need a car, go to Pattaya Rent a Car and ask for Shane." They come in, I'm not there because I'm not in the office day to day, and they mention my name hoping it'll knock something off the price.

Sometimes my staff message me, or tell me to check the CCTV because someone said they know me. I'm curious, so I look. More often than not, I've never seen the person in my life.

This isn't me thinking I'm someone everyone wants to know. It's just how it works when your name is on the business and people are trying their luck.

## What actually happens

My staff don't challenge anyone. If someone says they know me, it's really just a cue to go over and above with the service, because there's always a chance it gets back to me. I'd like to think they do that for everyone, but it's human nature to be extra careful when the customer might know the owner.

If they ask for a discount outright, the staff laugh it off like I would and give them a price. As a rule we don't do discounts. And honestly, when you're quoted a price at the counter, you wouldn't know whether it was discounted or not anyway. More in [renting a car in Pattaya](/articles/renting-a-car-in-pattaya).

## Why my friends pay full price

Even some of my very good friends don't get a discount. Nobody's being difficult about it. My staff know who my real friends are, and my real friends don't ask. They just pay the going rate.

I do the same. When I go to Koh Chang and stay at my friend's place, 15 Palms, I pay full price when I check out, and I normally overtip because it's my friend's business. More in [Koh Chang from Pattaya](/articles/koh-chang-from-pattaya).

That's the point. If you run a business and your friends do too, the best way to support each other is to pay your way. Friends don't expect discounts.
