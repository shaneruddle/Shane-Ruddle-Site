If business in Pattaya gets harder, the long-established businesses with a loyal customer base will still do okay. It's new businesses that will struggle. But a downturn isn't only a threat. It's also when the best opportunities appear. More in [why I think business in Pattaya is getting harder](/articles/why-i-think-business-in-pattaya-is-getting-harder).

## Newcomers will keep trying, and fail faster

Harder times won't stop people opening businesses. They'll just fail a lot quicker. On the way, they'll take a slice of everyone else's customers for a while, until they go under. More in [first mover, then five copycats](/articles/first-mover-then-five-copycats).

## Play defence, not attack

When things get tougher, I look at costs. Not necessarily headcount, but becoming more efficient: looking at every transaction and asking whether it's really necessary. I treat it as a chance to look at the business from a defensive point of view rather than an attacking one. More in [how I run seven businesses](/articles/how-i-run-seven-businesses).

## Downturns create opportunities

Markets have peaks and troughs, and if you're on the ground you know things should eventually come back. There's an old investing saying that the time to buy is when there's blood in the streets. If you keep your eyes open and your ear to the ground, you'll find opportunities.

That might mean buying an existing business, or buying a failing one for little more than the cost of its equipment, then relocating it or putting the kit in storage until better times. More in [valuing a business in Pattaya](/articles/valuing-a-business-in-pattaya) and [taking over a business in Pattaya](/articles/taking-over-a-business-in-pattaya).

## The same goes for property

The property market is flat at the moment, but people with cash are still making money. They're picking up really good deals, renting them out in the meantime, and waiting to sell at a profit when the market comes back. More in [the Pattaya property market](/articles/pattaya-property-market) and [is Pattaya overbuilt?](/articles/is-pattaya-overbuilt)

In a stable market there aren't as many opportunities. Any big disruption or swing is what creates them.
