Every year, high season arrives and Pattaya fills up with hundreds of thousands of visitors. At the peak, many of them have never travelled before, let alone been to Thailand. I call it silly season, because it's when you see the most surprising things.

Most of it is harmless, but some of it is avoidable. Here's what to expect, and how not to become one of the stories.

## Shirts off, flip-flops on

As I was driving recently, I passed a couple on a motorbike with helmets on, which is good, but no shirts, flip-flops and just a pair of shorts. If they come off, that's serious road rash.

Not surprisingly, road accidents go up in high season, and you see plenty of people walking around in bandages. Cover up when you ride, and wear proper shoes.

## Not knowing how the roads work

People step straight out into the road without looking, or simply don't know how traffic works here. Take your time, look both ways more than once, and don't assume anyone will stop for you. I've written more about that in [expect the unexpected crossing the road in Pattaya](/articles/expect-the-unexpected-crossing-the-road-in-pattaya).

## Not knowing the cannabis rules

Recently, we've seen a lot of visitors smoking cannabis without knowing what the law actually says. The rules have changed, and it isn't the free-for-all some people assume. Since 2025, cannabis flower can only be sold with a prescription, and shops aren't allowed to sell it for smoking on their premises. Check the current law before you do anything.

## Disputes over money

One story comes up pretty much every high season: a visitor complains they've been overcharged after a night out. These complaints usually don't get far, and some are probably just misunderstandings. Either way, agree on prices clearly up front.

## Sleeping it off outside 7-Eleven

Occasionally you'll see someone asleep outside a 7-Eleven, either too drunk to find their hotel or just too drunk full stop. It's still warm at night, so there's usually no drama, but it's not how anyone wants to remember their holiday.

## My take

High season is great for Pattaya, and most visitors have a fantastic time. A bit of common sense goes a long way: cover up on the bike, watch the road, know the law and agree on prices first. Enjoy it, just don't become part of silly season.
