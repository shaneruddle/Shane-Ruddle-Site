This probably won't make me popular with Pattaya's coworking spaces, but if you're a digital nomad or remote worker, I don't think you need one. Buy a coffee, find a seat, and nobody will bat an eyelid if you stay all day.

## Starbucks, Central Marina

My first recommendation is the Starbucks at Central Marina. Upstairs there's roughly 250 square metres of desks, tables and comfortable chairs. You buy a coffee and nobody tells you that you can't stay all day. There's a nice buzz of other people working too.

## Starbucks, The Little Walk

The Starbucks at The Little Walk on Sukhumvit Road works in a similar way. The front is busy, with people picking up coffees and Grab drivers coming and going. But the back is a separate area where basically everyone is just working.

To be honest, I'd recommend all of the Starbucks in Pattaya for this.

## Coffee Club and the rest

I've worked in Coffee Club branches too, and in plenty of other coffee shops around town. I've never had an issue anywhere. Most cafés here are happy for you to use their space as long as you buy a coffee, a juice or something to eat. If you don't mind a bit of noise, or you've got your headphones, the options open up even further.

## Hemingways Jomtien

I'll declare an interest here, because it's one of my own places. But I genuinely go and work at Hemingways Jomtien myself. It's a big restaurant, around 350 square metres, with lots of different areas where you can set yourself up for the day.

## Need a meeting space?

You don't need a coworking space for meetings either. There are sections at Hemingways Jomtien where you could hold a meeting without even booking, although booking ahead is a good idea. And I often see people holding staff meetings and catch-ups in Starbucks.

## Why I'd skip coworking spaces

In some countries, cafés charge you, or deliberately make it hard to stay all day. That just isn't the case in Pattaya. When you can work all day for the price of a coffee, I don't see much point in paying for a coworking space.

## My take

Pattaya is one of the easiest places in the world to work remotely. Buy a coffee, be considerate, and enjoy the buzz.
