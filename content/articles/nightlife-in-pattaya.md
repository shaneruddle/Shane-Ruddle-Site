In my younger years, a night out meant Walking Street. It's changed a lot since I arrived, and I haven't had a drink in over 10 years, so these days my evenings out look very different.

## Where do I go for a night out now?

A good restaurant, usually for a steak. My favourites:

- **Beefeater Steakhouse**, off Pattaya Second Road.
- **The Market**, just in front of Central Festival.
- **Gooseberries** in Pratumnak, which I've written about in [where a restaurant owner eats in Pattaya](/articles/where-a-restaurant-owner-eats-in-pattaya).

## Are the shows like Tiffany's worth it?

Yes, once. I went to Tiffany's when my parents were visiting. It's a very good show and truly unique, and I think it's worth the experience. It's not something I'd do again, though. Once is enough.

## If you do want a drink, where should you go?

If I had to go out drinking now, I'd skip Walking Street and go to Soi Buakhao.
