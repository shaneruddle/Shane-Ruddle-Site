If you want to play football in Pattaya, or want your kids to, Planet Football is where I'd start. I regularly take my boys there, and it caters for just about everyone, from four-year-olds to walking football. More in [is Pattaya good for families?](/articles/is-pattaya-good-for-families)

## Pattaya's first AstroTurf pitch

Planet Football is at Siam Country Club. As far as I know, it was the first AstroTurf football pitch in Pattaya, built around 15 years ago. It started with one pitch and now has two, plus a sports hall. Today there are probably more than 20 artificial pitches around Pattaya, but Planet Football was the first.

It's run by foreigners, it's very well organised and it's safe. It's not the cheapest, but I don't think most parents mind that, as long as the coaching is good, the atmosphere is friendly and the kids are safe.

## From age four to walking football

The youngest group is Mini Kicks, for roughly four to seven or eight-year-olds. From there, there are groups for every age, right up to veterans' leagues and even walking football.

There are different levels too:

- **Fun sessions** for kids who just want a kick-about.
- **Development classes** for more serious players. My boys sometimes do a development session as well as their regular one.
- **The academy**, by invitation, for the best young players, which I believe is sponsored.

## Leagues, an 11-a-side team and summer camps

As players get into their teens and young adulthood, there are Wednesday night leagues. Planet Football also has an 11-a-side team in the local league, playing against many of the Bangkok expat teams, and an 11-a-side pitch at a separate location.

They run summer camps too. My boys went to one this summer.

## Why we keep going

My kids absolutely love football. They talk about Ronaldo and Messi constantly and watch it on YouTube. At Planet Football they get to play it with other kids, in a safe, friendly place.

It's become part of our weekly routine, and it's not just for them. For me, it's a chance to socialise with the other mums and dads, and I genuinely enjoy going.

There's a personal side too. I played a lot of football as a kid in the UK. Then golf took over, and I barely played from about 15 to 30. When I came back to it in Pattaya, I played at Planet Football regularly. So in a way, my boys are following in my footsteps. More in [staying fit in Pattaya](/articles/staying-fit-in-pattaya).

Pattaya has a reputation for a lot of things, but a morning at Planet Football is the side most people never see: families, kids and a proper community around the game.
