One of the things I like most about doing business in Pattaya is how openly business owners share information. Even competitors. I don't know if that happens everywhere, but here very few people gatekeep. It feels like we're all on the same team. More in [why my best customers are other business owners](/articles/why-my-best-customers-are-other-business-owners).

## Rivals who compare notes

I'm friendly with a lot of the other car rental owners in Pattaya. We regularly ask each other how things are going. Are you finding it tough at the moment? Are all your cars out? That kind of talk is completely normal here. More in [16 years of Pattaya Rent a Car](/articles/16-years-of-pattaya-rent-a-car).

It works the same with restaurants. Recently I asked a fellow restaurateur how they were getting on. I told him one of our restaurants was about 10% down on revenue, and asked whether that was just us or the whole area. He was happy to tell me how his place was doing.

That kind of answer tells me whether I have a problem in my business, or whether the whole area is having a quiet patch.

## Advice goes both ways

Other owners often ask me for my opinion, and I ask them how they'd handle certain situations. If someone has dealt with a problem before, they'll usually tell you how.

## The real opponent is the conditions

I think it's because everyone knows how tough it is here. Quiet seasons, fewer tourists, rising costs. That's what we're all up against.

So it doesn't feel like we're competing against each other. It feels like we're all on the same team, fighting the conditions together. More in [how to survive the tougher years in Pattaya](/articles/how-to-survive-the-tougher-years-in-pattaya) and [why I think business in Pattaya is getting harder](/articles/why-i-think-business-in-pattaya-is-getting-harder).

## My advice

If you're opening a business here, get to know the other owners in your industry and your area. Ask how they're doing, and be honest when they ask you. You'll learn a lot from a five-minute chat with someone who's been through it.
