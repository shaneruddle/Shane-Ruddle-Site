We recently got a one-star review at Pattaya Rent a Car. Bad reviews happen, and some are fair. What frustrates me is when someone leaves out the facts that don't suit them, to make a business look far worse than it is. More in [customer service and online reviews](/articles/customer-service-and-online-reviews).

## What happened

A customer wanted to rent a motorbike but asked to test-ride it first. The staff were happy to let him, with no obligation to rent. He rode it for about 15 minutes, came back and said it was too old.

We offered him a newer bike, but newer bikes cost more. He said that was too expensive and he'd take his business elsewhere. The staff told him that was entirely his choice.

In his review, he said the staff were hostile. They weren't. I know my staff. I think he simply didn't get what he wanted, which was probably the newer bike at the older bike's price, and our pricing doesn't work that way. More in [scooter or car in Pattaya?](/articles/scooter-or-car-in-pattaya)

## How I replied

Like I always do, I wrote my reply for everyone reading it, not just for him. I kept it professional and stuck to the facts. I made it clear that his account left out key information, and that it was very different from my staff's account of what happened. More in [why Pattaya's expat Facebook groups are so toxic](/articles/why-pattaya-expat-facebook-groups-are-so-toxic).

## My advice to other owners

**Accept that some are unavoidable.** Some people don't like it when they don't get their way, a bit like a child throwing a tantrum. In the early days of Pattaya Rent a Car, one-star reviews used to upset me a lot. Now I know some are simply impossible to avoid, because some expectations are impossible to meet.

It's like someone seeing fish and chips on the menu at ฿350, asking to pay ฿150, then leaving one star because you said no. Thankfully it's only a very small percentage of customers.

**Check the reviewer's history.** I always look at what else they've reviewed. It's amazing how often someone who leaves us one star has given one star to almost everyone.
