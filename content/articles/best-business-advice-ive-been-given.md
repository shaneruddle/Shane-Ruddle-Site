I've had plenty of advice over 20 years in business. Two pieces changed how I think, and there's a third I'd pass on to anyone.

## "You'll be surprised how quickly things turn around"

When Fairways Golf Range failed, I was mentally exhausted and ready to leave Thailand and go home. Some of it was my own doing, but a lot wasn't, so I felt hard done by. I felt I'd let everyone down: the staff, the customers, and my parents, who'd lent me the money. More in [my biggest business failure](/articles/my-biggest-business-failure).

Mark, one of my business partners, told me I'd be surprised how quickly things could turn around, and how soon the setback would be in my rear-view mirror.

He was right. Within months I'd stopped dwelling on it and was pushing forward with Pattaya Rent a Car. Within a couple of years I'd paid back the money I'd lost, I was on a much better footing, and I was in a better frame of mind because of that failure. Things can turn around quickly if you keep knocking on the door. More in [16 years of Pattaya Rent a Car](/articles/16-years-of-pattaya-rent-a-car).

## "You can be kind and still be respected"

The second came from a friend, and it changed how I treat my Thai staff. Early on I wasn't sympathetic towards them. I'd been let down many times and had built up some resentment. I saw them as employees, almost as a means to an end, not as people. I also believed that showing kindness meant showing weakness.

He opened my eyes to treating staff with respect, as people, and showed me you can be kind and still earn their respect. More in [what makes a good manager in Pattaya](/articles/what-makes-a-good-manager-in-pattaya).

## What I'd pass on: persist, and choose your hard

Persistence beats resistance. In business you will meet resistance, some of it much stronger than the rest. It goes with the territory. Keep going.

And try to enjoy it. Life is hard. Working for someone else is hard. Having no job is hard. Owning your own business is hard. So choose your hard, own it, take responsibility for it and get on with it. More in [things I'd never do again in business](/articles/things-id-never-do-again-in-business).
