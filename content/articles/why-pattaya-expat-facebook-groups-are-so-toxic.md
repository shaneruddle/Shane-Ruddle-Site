Pattaya's expat Facebook groups can be a toxic place. My managers and I don't engage in them, especially the food groups. But I'd still tell newcomers to join them, as long as they read them the right way. More in [customer service and online reviews](/articles/customer-service-and-online-reviews).

## Why they're so negative

I think there are three reasons:

- **Too much time.** Pattaya has a lot of retirees with too much time on their hands, and some of them just like to troll and get a reaction.
- **People enjoy seeing others fail.** You don't get much positivity or support in these groups. It's rare for people to come out and back a business.
- **Anonymity.** Facebook makes it easy to post under a fake profile, or anonymously from a real one.

## Why we don't engage

Post a promotion and there'll always be negative remarks. It's draining, and you quickly realise you can't educate anyone on there. People come at you from a completely different point of view, and even when you make your point well, they rarely give in. It also takes up a lot of time. More in [marketing a small business in Pattaya](/articles/marketing-a-small-business-in-pattaya).

Has it cost us customers? Possibly. But if someone puts too much weight on what strangers post on Facebook, they may not be the customers we're looking for anyway.

## "It's not for you"

A lot of people here assume every business wants them as a customer. Imagine someone walking into a Ferrari dealership and telling the salesman the car is too expensive. The salesman would say, "Yes, but it's not for you."

If Hemingways is too expensive for you, maybe you're not our customer. I think some of the attacks come from people who can't afford a venue and take that personally, as if knocking it might bring the prices down. More in [where a restaurant owner eats in Pattaya](/articles/where-a-restaurant-owner-eats-in-pattaya).

## My advice to business owners

If you feel you have to reply, remember who you're really talking to. Don't try to win over the person attacking you. Write your reply for everyone else reading it.

## My advice to newcomers

Join them. There's useful information on there, it's a good way to find out what's going on, and you'll find some valid points of view. But take everything with a pinch of salt, and look at who is commenting rather than just what they're saying.
