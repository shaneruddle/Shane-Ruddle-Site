For me, there's no comparison. Christmas in England is excellent, and nothing in Pattaya can replace it. But expats here make a real effort to recreate it, and if you go about it the right way, you can still have a good Christmas Day in the sun.

## Christmas back home

The UK is a fantastic place to spend Christmas. It's cold, everyone's in a good mood, people take time off work, the shops are busy, and there are lights, music and Santa everywhere. If you get a snowy one, even better.

## How expats recreate it here

You can't recreate the weather, but you can recreate most of the rest. A lot of expats book Christmas lunch at a British pub and celebrate there with family.

We do that at each of our Hemingways pubs. There's turkey, Christmas music, and usually someone dressed as Santa handing out presents to the kids. It's a lovely atmosphere, and we're normally fully booked. This isn't a plug, but wherever you go, book in advance.

Then you walk out of the pub, and it's boiling hot and everyone's in shorts and flip-flops.

## Behind the scenes on Christmas Day

For restaurant staff, Christmas Day is one of the busiest of the year. I used to work it myself at the Sportsman, and it's a real relief when it's over.

The pressure is the expectations. You're charging a lot more than usual, and you really don't want to get it wrong and ruin someone's Christmas Day. So the staff work extra hard. We pay them accordingly and give them bonuses, and people tend to tip generously over the holidays. A lot of the food prep is done beforehand, so as long as it's properly planned, it's busy but under control.

At our other businesses it's different. For the car rental staff, it's just another working day. The property staff usually take it off, because being open isn't as critical for them.

## Pattaya goes all out on decorations

You won't miss Christmas in Pattaya. The malls, hotels and restaurants all put up tinsel and decorations, and big shopping centres like Central and Terminal 21 have a giant Christmas tree.

It's not a Thai holiday, but Thais, certainly in Bangkok and Pattaya, embrace pretty much every celebration going: Chinese New Year, Christmas, Valentine's Day, Halloween. Any excuse to have some fun, and they'll take it.

## My take

We make the most of it, and we try our best to recreate what we have back home. It can be a really nice day. But a hot Christmas in flip-flops will never quite replace a cold one in England.
