AI is going to change every small business in Pattaya, and it's already changing mine. If you don't start using it, a competitor will eventually take some of your market share. I think that's inevitable. More in [how I use AI in my businesses](/articles/how-i-use-ai-in-my-businesses).

## What it takes over first

The mundane stuff goes first: admin, and some basic marketing.

At Pattaya Rent a Car, AI already gives customers instant replies to basic questions. Are you open today? What are your opening hours? What cars have you got available? Customers get an answer straight away, at any time of day.

## Hiring less, not firing

So far I haven't let anyone go because of AI, and I'm not at that stage. What I'm seeing instead is less need to hire. My businesses are getting more done with the same number of staff.

## "It's not for me"

If you think AI is too complicated or not relevant to your business, think back to when the internet first arrived. Some owners didn't think they needed a website, and they lost ground. This is the same. As a business owner, I don't think you've got a choice. There may be a few businesses that really don't need it, but I can't think of one.

## Where to start

Start simple. Just chat with ChatGPT or Claude, try their voice mode, and use them to search for things. The point at first isn't to automate anything. It's to understand what AI can do and how quickly things are changing.
