At Pattaya Rent a Car, we hold on to our cars a lot longer than many rental companies in other countries would. Part of that is simple economics. But there's more to it than that.

## Some customers prefer an older car

Believe it or not, plenty of customers don't actually want a brand new car. The roads here are chaotic, and there's a fair chance of picking up a scrape, usually something minor, but a scrape all the same. The thought of scratching a brand new car doesn't sit well with a lot of people.

An older car takes that worry away, and it's cheaper too, which suits more price-conscious customers.

## Buy cars that are easy to keep running

Older cars normally mean more mechanical problems, and that's true. But if you buy smart, you can reduce a lot of that risk. Most of our fleet is Toyota, and in Thailand Toyotas are easy and cheap to maintain and repair. So you don't get the usual problems of an older car nearly as much here.

## No hiding the age

We're completely open about how old each car is. When you book on our website, you can choose between newer and older cars, and we even show the year. Nothing is hidden.

## How we know it's time to sell

It isn't based on a set age. It comes down to the customer and the staff.

Even when people know they're getting an older car, a rental does a lot of kilometres, so sometimes it looks older than they expected. When the staff start getting complaints about a car, and customers don't feel they've had value for money, that's the first sign.

The second, and the biggest red flag, is when the staff start to feel a bit embarrassed handing the car over. When that happens, we move the car on. The same goes for our motorbikes.

## My take

A newer car isn't always what customers want. Be honest about what you're renting, buy cars that are easy to look after, and listen to your staff. They'll tell you when it's time to let one go.
