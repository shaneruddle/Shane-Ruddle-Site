People leave Thailand thinking the grass is greener. In my experience, almost all of them come back. By all means go and have a look around, but there's a pattern I've seen for 20 years.

## The friend who keeps coming back

I've known one friend for about 20 years. He's worked for me, and I've got him two or three other jobs over the years. His habit is always the same: he comes to Thailand, works for a couple of years, then decides the grass is greener and leaves. Back to the UK, to America, to other parts of the world.

The last time, he worked for me for a couple of years, decided the role wasn't for him, and went off to work exhibitions around the world. Europe, America, Australia, other parts of Asia, for about two years.

I met up with him recently. He said: "I don't know why I keep doing this. Every time I leave to start something somewhere else, I always come back to Pattaya. Everywhere I go just doesn't feel like home like Pattaya does."

He says he's back for good this time. Knowing him, he may wander off again. But I'd bet on him coming back.

## It's not just him

Another friend spent around 20 years living in Japan and absolutely loves it. He recently went to Australia to see his kids, had a really good time, and still missed Thailand while he was there. More in [Thailand vs Japan](/articles/thailand-vs-japan).

For whatever reason, the draw is here. And I think the longer you stay, the stronger it gets.

## What pulls people back

- **The way of life.** It's easy to do things here and easy to get around.
- **Value for money.** Everywhere is expensive now. Thailand is getting dearer too, but compared with what you get elsewhere it's still relatively cheap. More in [is Pattaya expensive?](/articles/is-pattaya-expensive)
- **Service.** What you get for your money here beats what you'd get elsewhere for a lot more.
- **The basics.** The people, the food, the weather.
- **The community.** There's a big expat community and a network of friends. If you've lived here a few years, you've built one, and that's a big part of what makes it feel like home.

## Go and look, but you'll probably be back

I'm not saying never leave. If you think the grass is greener, go and see for yourself. But over the years, nearly everyone I've known who's done that has ended up wanting to come back to Thailand.
