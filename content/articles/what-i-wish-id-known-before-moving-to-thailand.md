I've been in Thailand for around 20 years now. Looking back, there are a few things I wish I'd known when I first arrived.

## What did I expect, and what did I find?

I expected somewhere much less developed. I knew I'd experience a different country and a different culture, but I wasn't expecting all the comforts I had back home as well, like the food. I just didn't expect it to be comparable to the West.

## What do I wish someone had told me?

How much I was going to enjoy it here. If I'd known, I would have treated it as my home from the start and put down roots earlier.

Instead, I spent a bit too long in tourist party mode and didn't worry much about jeopardising my life here. Now I'm very protective of it.

## What would I have done differently with money?

Not a lot, because I didn't have much back then. I've always been fairly good with money and quite frugal, but I spent what I earned. I probably spent a bit too much on entertainment, golf and going out, and not enough on reinvesting.

## What surprised me about Thai people?

How welcoming and accommodating they are, and above all how tolerant. Some visitors can be aggressive or overbearing, which is the very opposite of Thai culture, and Thais are remarkably good at dealing with it.

## My advice if you're thinking of moving here

Do it. Absolutely do it, and fully commit to it.

It depends on your finances, of course. If you have a passive income from home, such as a pension or other investments, and enough to live a reasonable lifestyle here, I'd absolutely do it.

That's not to knock the West, and it isn't true of everywhere or everyone. But for me, the way of life here is so good compared with the rat race back home.
