You often hear that in Thailand the foreigner always pays after an accident. In my experience, that's a myth. What does happen is an unwritten rule that the bigger vehicle usually pays. Quite often that's a foreigner in a car and a Thai on a motorbike, which is where the myth comes from. More in [driving in Pattaya](/articles/driving-in-pattaya).

## The bigger vehicle pays

I don't know if it's official, but that's what I've seen, and so have plenty of friends. If you're in a car and the other person is on a motorbike, you'll often end up paying. Whether that applies to Thai drivers in cars too, I honestly don't know. More in [scooter or car in Pattaya?](/articles/scooter-or-car-in-pattaya)

## A real example

One of our customers braked fairly hard at a set of traffic lights that had turned red, and a motorbike went into the back of the car. It happened right next to a police box, so an officer came out and saw the whole thing.

The damage was only about ฿2,000 to ฿3,000. The officer suggested everyone just go their separate ways rather than file reports and involve insurance. Our customer called us, and we agreed and covered it ourselves.

We could have gone through the full process, and the rider would have been made to pay. But he clearly didn't have much money, so it would likely have meant instalments and a lot of hassle for a few thousand baht. Sometimes it's better to accept it and move on. ## What to do after an accident

1. **If it's a rental car, call the rental company first.** We'll guide you through it.
2. **Check everyone is okay.** If anyone is hurt, call for medical help straight away: 1669 for an ambulance, 191 for the police, or 1155 for the Tourist Police, who speak English.
3. **Wait at the roadside and call your insurer.** The assessor usually arrives by motorbike, often within an hour, with a clipboard. They'll ask what happened and decide who was at fault.
4. **With first-class insurance, your own damage is covered** whoever was at fault.
5. **Drive on or get towed.** If the car can't be driven, wait for a tow truck and arrange a replacement car.
