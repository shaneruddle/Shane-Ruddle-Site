Not long ago, if someone wanted to start a small business in Pattaya, they opened a beer bar. Now, more and more, they open a coffee shop. It's a shift I've watched happen over the last ten years or so, and I don't think it's going anywhere.

## The pub has become the coffee shop

Back in the UK, the pub was never just a place to drink. It was the social centre of a lot of villages and communities. Many of those pubs have closed now, and in a lot of places coffee shops have taken over that role.

Part of it is that fewer people in the West are drinking alcohol. But people still need somewhere to meet, talk and be around others. Coffee shops have become the new social club.

## The same thing is happening in Pattaya

It used to feel like every other business in Pattaya sold alcohol. That's slowly changing. Not as far as it has in the West, but the direction is clear. Fewer people are drinking, and they still want somewhere to socialise.

If you wanted to start a business here before, a beer bar was the obvious choice. For anyone who doesn't know, a beer bar is exactly what it sounds like: a simple bar selling beer, usually with no food. It was relatively cheap to set up.

Now the coffee shop is taking its place. It's a bit more involved to open than a beer bar, but that's the trend I'm seeing.

## Is there still room for more?

There are a lot of coffee shops already. But I think there's still growth to come.

As life becomes more digital, with more people on their phones and working from home, we still need human contact, whether that's with staff or other customers. Coffee shops are where that happens now. The big chains clearly think so too. Starbucks Thailand is aiming for 600 stores by early 2027, and Café Amazon is targeting around 300 new outlets a year, even if weak consumer confidence has slowed it down a little.

## My advice if you're opening one

A generic coffee shop is very competitive, and there's a good chance it will fail. You need to stand out.

- **Find a niche.** Look at what's trending in coffee shops around the world, and see what isn't being done here yet.
- **Specialise.** Speciality coffee, a particular style or a strong theme can set you apart.
- **Think about food.** Good food alongside the coffee gives people a reason to stay, and to come back.

The beer bar was the easy way into business in Pattaya. The coffee shop is the new version, but it's only worth doing if you give people a reason to choose yours.
