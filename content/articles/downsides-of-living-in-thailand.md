I've lived in Thailand for more than 20 years, and I love it here. But nowhere is perfect. These are three things I genuinely miss about the UK.

## 1. Food variety

It's not the quality of the food. It's the variety. When I walk into a supermarket back in the UK, the choice of the food I like to eat is huge.

You don't get that here, for an obvious reason. Thai cuisine is very different from Western food, and Westerners are a small share of the population, so supermarkets are mostly stocked for Thai shoppers. That makes complete sense, and you can find British favourites in the expat shops if you're happy to pay for them. But I don't eat much Thai food, so I really miss the everyday variety back home. I've written more about that in [what I miss about the UK](/articles/what-i-miss-about-the-uk).

## 2. The heat, all year round

When you first arrive, the weather is brilliant because it never stops you doing anything. As a keen golfer back in the UK, the weather decided whether I played. Here, it never did.

What the heat does do is drain you. It might not stop you playing golf, but it can make golf a lot less fun when you're exhausted by the heat.

## 3. The air

It isn't really the burning season. Pattaya doesn't get that the way Chiang Mai does, mainly because we're by the sea and get the wind.

It's more that hot, humid air never feels as fresh as a crisp summer's morning in the UK. I'm a runner, and a couple of summers ago I went back and did a 10k that felt effortless compared with running here.

## My take

None of these are reasons to leave. They're just the things I miss from home. I'd take the heat and the limited supermarket shelves for everything else Thailand gives me, but it's worth knowing what you'll miss before you move.
