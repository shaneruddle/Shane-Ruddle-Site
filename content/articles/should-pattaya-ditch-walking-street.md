Some people say Pattaya should get rid of Walking Street and its image. I say no. There's nothing wrong with having an entertainment area in a big city. It just needs to be kept in its own zone, so if you don't want to see it, you don't have to. More in [Walking Street, Pattaya](/articles/walking-street-pattaya).

## Zones, not bans

Pattaya was a bit late to this, but over the last ten years or so the city has tried to concentrate nightlife into certain areas. It never used to be like that. As I understand it, the main entertainment zones are Walking Street, the Soi Buakhao area and Sois 6, 7 and 8. I'd welcome that approach in any city. More in [nightlife in Pattaya](/articles/nightlife-in-pattaya).

## It doesn't put people off

Walking Street is one street in a big city. Families don't tend to go into Central Pattaya, and certainly not near Walking Street. I don't think anyone with an open mind would refuse to buy property here because of it. That would be like refusing to buy in London because you support Manchester United. More in [is Pattaya good for families?](/articles/is-pattaya-good-for-families) and [the best area to live in Pattaya](/articles/best-area-to-live-in-pattaya).

## What really holds Pattaya back

I don't see Walking Street as seedy, and I don't think it's what stops Pattaya being a family destination. You can have an entertainment area and still work towards a more family-friendly city. The two can sit side by side.

The real barriers are road safety, how hard it is to walk around, and how dirty parts of the city are. Fix those and Pattaya becomes far more family-friendly, with or without Walking Street. More in [my tips for the road in Pattaya](/articles/my-tips-for-the-road-in-pattaya) and [if I were mayor of Pattaya](/articles/if-i-were-mayor-of-pattaya).

## What I'd change

I'd enforce the zones properly. There are still entertainment venues outside them, and I'd say nothing outside those zones can operate in that industry.

I'd also make the zones less visible. If you drive past Soi 6, you can see everything going on down the street. I don't think that should be allowed. It should be there if you go looking for it, not in everyone's face. More in [why Pattaya's old reputation is out of date](/articles/pattaya-old-reputation-out-of-date).
