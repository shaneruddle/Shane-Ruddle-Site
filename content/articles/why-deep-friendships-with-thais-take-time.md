I've lived in Thailand for more than 20 years, and if I'm honest, I have fewer than half a dozen deep friendships with Thais. This isn't a criticism of anyone. It's just how it is when you live in a country with a different language and culture.

## How those friendships formed

The Thai friends I'm close to all work with me. Those friendships formed through longevity: knowing each other for a long time, with our personal lives slowly creeping into our professional ones. It's taken years.

With a fellow Westerner, I could probably build the same kind of friendship in a twentieth of the time.

## Language, culture and shared interests

The reasons are simple: language, culture and interests. Say there's a big world event, like a war somewhere. Most of the Thais I know in Pattaya wouldn't want a long conversation about it, because it isn't part of their daily life and they feel it doesn't affect them. Different interests, that's all.

So most of my deep conversations, and my deepest friendships, are with Westerners.

## It isn't about Thailand

I want to be clear that this isn't a criticism of Thais. It would be the same anywhere with a very different language and culture. If I moved to Japan, I'm sure I'd find the same thing. If I moved to Australia or America, I'm confident I'd make deep friendships quickly, because we'd share a language and interests.

It just takes a lot more effort to build a deep friendship across a language and a culture.

## If you want deeper Thai friendships

You have to really immerse yourself in the way of life and the culture. And absolutely learn Thai.

That doesn't mean every friendship has to be in Thai. But learning the language helps you understand how things work here, how people think and who they are. That gives you a much better footing to build something deeper. More in [learning Thai](/articles/learning-thai).

## My take

Don't be disappointed if your closest friends here end up being other foreigners. That's normal. But if you want deeper Thai friendships, give it time, put in the effort, and learn the language.
