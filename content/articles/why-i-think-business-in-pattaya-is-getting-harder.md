I think doing business in Pattaya is going to get harder over the next few years. If someone asked me today whether to start a business here, I'd say no. I'd only look at buying one. More in [why tourism in Thailand is down](/articles/why-tourism-in-thailand-is-down).

## The economic headwind

The first worry is the big picture, and there's nothing any of us can do about it. If fewer people come to the city, we're all fighting over a smaller pie. If tourism is down 10%, it's very hard to grow your business by 10%, because you're starting from behind. More in [Pattaya's high season is getting shorter](/articles/pattayas-high-season-is-getting-shorter).

## AI and jobs

The second worry is AI. I'm leaning into it hard in my own businesses, but that doesn't help the average person. It may help me as an owner, but it doesn't help my staff.

I think the job market here may start to feel the effects, and, dare I say it, we could even see some unemployment. Thailand doesn't have the same social safety net as many Western countries, so I fear for Thai workers especially. More in [how AI will change small businesses in Pattaya](/articles/how-ai-will-change-small-businesses-in-pattaya).

## Why I'm not hiring

I'm not looking to let anyone go, but I'm not planning to take on more staff over the next few years either. Partly that's the economic outlook. Partly it's that whenever a new job comes up, I first ask whether it can be done with automation. More in [getting my staff to use AI](/articles/getting-my-staff-to-use-ai).

## Would I start a business now?

No. Even myself, I'd only look at buying an existing business. Starting from scratch against these headwinds would be very hard. More in [why buy a business in Pattaya](/articles/why-buy-a-business-in-pattaya).

That doesn't mean nobody can make money. Even in a falling stock market, some people do well. But think of the S&P 500: when all 500 companies are making money, it's easy to make money. When half of them aren't, it's a lot harder. I feel we're heading into that kind of period, where the odds of making money are going down.
