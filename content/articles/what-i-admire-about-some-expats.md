I've written about the [expat habits that drive me mad](/articles/things-expats-do-that-drive-me-mad). To be fair, there's plenty I admire too.

## The charity work

A lot of Pattaya's charities are driven by expats. The Father Ray Foundation has been doing incredible work for decades. Hand to Hand works with underprivileged children and families, and there's also the Mercy Centre.

A newer one I've backed is Helmet Heroes, run by a man called Rick. He buys helmets and stands at the roadside, usually alongside the police when they stop riders without helmets, and hands them out free. It's smaller than the big charities, which do far more, but road safety is something I'm passionate about, so it was an easy one to support. More in [my tips for the road in Pattaya](/articles/my-tips-for-the-road-in-pattaya).

## Quiet acts of kindness

You don't have to run a charity to do good. For years there was a man I'd often see around town with a bag and a litter picker, cleaning up every day. Litter is top of my list in [if I were mayor of Pattaya](/articles/if-i-were-mayor-of-pattaya).

I also admire foreigners who support their partner's Thai family because they want to, not because they're told to or because it's tradition. Often it's a genuine relationship between people closer in age, and the foreigner simply has some money to spare and helps out of the goodness of their heart. More in [relationships in Pattaya](/articles/relationships-in-pattaya).

## The few who speak fluent Thai

Anyone who learns Thai properly has my respect. It's tonal and very hard. Most long-term expats, me included, speak some Thai, but in 20 years I've only met about a dozen friends and colleagues I'd call fluent.

One of them is my business partner Chris, who's French. He speaks the best Thai of all my friends and partners. When he gets going, I haven't a clue what he's saying, and you have to admire that. More in [learning Thai](/articles/learning-thai). For the opposite, read [why some expats turn bitter about Thailand](/articles/why-some-expats-turn-bitter-about-thailand).
