AI will never replace a property agent. But an agent who uses AI could easily replace one who doesn't. Here's how my agents at Alan Bolton and East Coast Real Estate use it. More in [working as a property agent in Pattaya](/articles/working-as-a-property-agent-in-pattaya).

## How my agents use it

The basics, mostly. They use AI to help answer enquiries, improve listings, write social media posts and come up with content ideas.

We used to have a separate tool for turning property listings into social media posts. Now that's built straight into our back-end system, so it's just part of the everyday workflow.

## Why it won't replace agents

People will always want to view a property before they decide, and AI can't do that. Property is also a relationship business. Buyers and sellers want someone they trust, and AI can't be that person.

## Why it still matters

If you're not a particularly good agent and you're up against someone using AI well, you don't stand a chance. I think that's true in every industry. AI isn't going to take your job, but someone using AI could. More in [how AI will change small businesses in Pattaya](/articles/how-ai-will-change-small-businesses-in-pattaya).

## The habit I want every new agent to have

Just play around with it. Every time you hit a problem at work, ask yourself: can I use AI to solve this?

And if the same problem comes up more than about three times, definitely look for a way to solve it or automate it with AI. That's my rule of three. More in [the biggest mistakes people make with AI](/articles/biggest-mistakes-people-make-with-ai).
