When I first took up golf, I didn't realise how much of a mental game it is. It took me years to understand that, and it's turned out to be one of the most useful lessons I've taken into business.

## Stay in the moment

In golf, you have to stay in the moment. You can't let a great shot carry your emotions away, and you can't let a bad shot or a bad hole wreck your whole round. Whichever end of the emotional scale you're at, the job is to stay calm and collected and play the next shot.

You see it with the professionals. Most of them barely show any emotion until a really special moment, often right at the end of the round, when some even break down in tears. Up until then, their emotions are playing havoc inside them, and they're working hard to keep a lid on it, not getting too excited and not getting too down.

## Business is the same

In business, it happens every week. Some weeks it's every day, and some days it's every hour, so I won't single out one example. You get dragged into situations, some of them very personal, and it's very hard to keep your cool the whole time.

But if you don't, it's just like golf. The situation in front of you spills over into the next decision, the way a bad shot spills into the next one. Then you can start to spiral, and one incident turns into something much bigger than it ever should have been.

## How I reset

**Zoom out.** In the middle of a bad round or a bad run, it's very hard to tell whether you're making any progress. Sometimes it feels like you're going backwards. Business is the same, and life is too. But if you look back over a year, or six years, you realise you have made progress.

**Take a break.** In sport, and golf in particular, more practice and more playing doesn't always make you better. Sometimes a step back works best. When you come back, you have a fresh set of eyes, maybe some new ideas and a different approach. Business is no different.

## My take

Don't let one good or bad moment decide the next one. Stay in the moment, zoom out when it feels like you're going backwards, and don't be afraid to step away for a while.
