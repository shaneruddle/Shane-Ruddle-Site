I run restaurants myself, so people often ask where I eat when I'm not at my own places. Here are my go-to spots in Pattaya, and what I look for in any restaurant.

## Marco's: my favourite Italian in Pattaya

Marco's is an Italian restaurant on Thappraya Road. I like the pizza and I like the pasta, but the thing I'd single out is the dessert trio: three or four miniature desserts, such as a chocolate mousse and a little crème brûlée. It's a really nice touch.

It's also very well managed, and it's owner-operated. That's often a major advantage. In my experience, a lot of the most successful restaurants in Pattaya are run by their owners.

## Gooseberries: where I go for a steak

Gooseberries in Pratumnak is my go-to for a steak. It's owned by a friend of mine, Kenneth. The restaurant is only about a year old, but Kenneth is a chef himself and has plenty of experience, and it shows.

## Where do I eat Thai food in Pattaya?

Honestly, I don't eat much Thai food here. I like it, but almost every restaurant in Pattaya has Western options too, and I always go for those. Most of my Thai food is eaten when I'm travelling around the country and end up somewhere more remote.

If I do want Thai food in Pattaya, I'd head up Pratumnak Hill towards Cosy Beach, to places like The Forest by the Sky or The Sky Gallery.

## How to tell if a restaurant is good

As a restaurant owner, these are the three things I notice.

**1. Cleanliness.** First impressions count. Look up at the light bulbs and the fans. If you can see dust, the place could be better.

**2. The staff.** Watch their attitude. Do they look happy at work, or overworked and tired? If they look worn out, that can be a sign something is going on behind the scenes.

**3. The food meets expectations.** When the meal arrives, is it well presented and what you'd expect? It doesn't have to be first-class food. If you go into a pub, you expect pub food. What matters is that your expectations are met.
