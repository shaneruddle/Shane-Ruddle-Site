We regularly get contacted by owners who want to sell a property they didn't buy through us. I know some agency owners turn that business away, on the basis of "why aren't you going back to the agent who sold it to you?" I don't. Here's why sellers move on, and how we handle it.

## Anyone can sell you a condo. Few can sell it for you

As I've said before, buying in Thailand is easy and [selling is hard](/articles/selling-a-condo-in-pattaya). To keep the numbers simple: there might be a thousand agencies that could help you buy a property, but maybe only ten that could actually sell it for you. Selling takes far more work.

So people often buy through the first agency they come across, because the process is easy. When they go back to that agency to sell, it may not be set up for it, or big enough to help.

## An awkward conversation

There's another reason. If someone wants to sell a few years after buying, and it wasn't planned as a flip, there's an argument that they didn't buy well, and they may quietly blame the agent who sold it to them. Going back to say "now I want to sell" can be uncomfortable. Why have that conversation when you can just pass it to someone else?

## Or they've gone to everyone

It's also possible they did go back to their buying agent. Because selling is so hard, and there's no exclusivity here, many owners simply list with every agency they can.

## Why we take them on

We usually say yes. It's another property on our database, and you never know when an enquiry will come in for it. It's also another property owner, and we get a lot of repeat business. It would be foolish to dismiss it.

Listing is rarely much work. Because owners want to list with several agencies, they usually take the photos and write the descriptions themselves.

## When we say no

There are a few times we push back:

- **It's a long way to get to.** If we'd have to travel far to see it, we may pass.
- **It isn't ready to sell.** If there are complications, like the property being in probate, we tell the owner to come back when they're in a position to sell.
- **We're on the second list.** If we've already seen it online with half a dozen or a dozen agencies, and a month later we get an email asking us to list it too, the answer is usually no.

## My take

If you bought through one agency and want to sell through another, that's completely normal, and most agencies will be glad to have you. Just be ready to do some of the work yourself, and pick agencies that are actually set up to sell.
