I went to Dubai with my family last year, and I'd recommend it to anyone with children. It's safe, spectacular and packed with things to do. But it's a holiday for me, not somewhere I'd live. Thailand gives you more, for a lot less.

## What Dubai does brilliantly

It's very safe. Everything is grand and spectacular, from the buildings to the activities. If you've got kids, it's a must for three days to a week, with theme parks, water parks and plenty more. More in [is Pattaya good for families?](/articles/is-pattaya-good-for-families)

## Why I wouldn't live there

This is just my opinion, but the parts I saw felt very new and polished, almost too clean, a bit clinical. I didn't feel the depth, history and traditions that you feel everywhere in Thailand. Dubai has its own Emirati heritage, but as a visitor I mostly saw the modern side.

We found the locals very friendly and welcoming. But for me, nothing beats Thai hospitality. Give me Thailand's warmth any day of the week.

## Ferrari vs tuk-tuk

The best way I can sum it up is the traffic. In Dubai, you see supercars everywhere, Ferraris and the like. In Bangkok, you see tuk-tuks. A Ferrari is very impressive. But there's something about a tuk-tuk ride, raw, noisy and a bit messy, that feels like the real Thailand. Dubai is the Ferrari. Thailand is the tuk-tuk, and I'll take the tuk-tuk. More in [getting around Pattaya](/articles/getting-around-pattaya).

## It's expensive

One family breakfast out came to about £100, roughly ฿4,000. The same meal in one of my Pattaya restaurants would have been no more than ฿1,500.

Our hotel was five-star, but in my view it didn't compare with a five-star hotel in Thailand. Thai hotels tend to sit on far more land, so you get more space and better facilities for your money. More in [where to stay in Pattaya](/articles/where-to-stay-in-pattaya) and [is Pattaya expensive?](/articles/is-pattaya-expensive)

If you live and work in Dubai, there's no personal income tax, so you may have more to spend. But coming from Thailand, you'll find it expensive. To be honest, you'll find most places expensive compared with Thailand.
